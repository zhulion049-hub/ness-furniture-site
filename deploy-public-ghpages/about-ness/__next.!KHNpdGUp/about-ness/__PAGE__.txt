Our manufacturing capabilities cover upholstered furniture, tables, casegoods, beds, dining furniture, outdoor furniture and customized project pieces, together with specialized collections in microcement, concrete and mosaic.

From a drawing, reference image or material concept, our team helps turn design intent into manufacturable furniture through technical review, material development, sampling, production, quality control and project-specific packing. We believe good manufacturing is not simply about making furniture look right—it is about understanding the design, communicating clearly, building it reliably and delivering what was approved.

Whether for a hotel, residence, commercial project, private-label collection or customized furniture program, NESS aims to be the manufacturing partner behind the design: transparent in communication,
