OEM & ODM — What's the Difference?

OEM manufacturing is suited to clients who already have a product design, drawing, specification, or reference and need a manufacturing partner to produce it according to approved requirements. ODM manufacturing is suited to clients who need support developing or adapting a product. NESS can work from an existing product, reference image, sketch, concept, or initial specification and support the process from technical development and material selection to sampling and production. Both models can be adapted to different project requirements, production quantities, materials, finishes, and packaging specifications.

From Reference to Production

How We Solve OEM & ODM Manufacturing Challenges

OEM and ODM projects often involve more than simply producing a finished product. Designs may need to be reviewed for manufacturability, materials may need to be matched or substituted, and prototypes may require several rounds of adjustment before production. NESS connects product development with manufacturing through a structured process. We review drawings, dimensions, materials, construction, and finishing requirements before sampling. After the prototype is evaluated and approved, the specifications are transferred into batch production, with quality checks throughout manufacturing and before shipment. This approach helps reduce communication gaps between design and production while keeping the approved product specifications consistent from sample to final delivery.

Our OEM & ODM Process

01 — Drawing & Reference Review Review drawings, dimensions, reference products, images, and project requirements. 02 — Material & Finish Confirmation Confirm fabrics, wood, metal, stone, colors, finishes, hardware, and other specifications. 03 — Prototype & Sampling Develop the first sample or prototype for evaluation and adjustment. 04 — Approval & Production Preparation Confirm approved specifications, production details, and quality requirements before batch manufacturing. 05 — Batch Production Manufacture according to the approved sample, drawings, materials, and specifications. 06 — Quality Control Inspect dimensions, structure, materials, finishes, assembly, and overall product quality. 07 — Packing & Container Loading Products are packed according to project or customer requirements and prepared for shipment.
