Hospitality Projects Require More Than Furniture

Hospitality Projects Require More Than Furniture

Hotel and hospitality projects often involve multiple furniture categories, materials, finishes, dimensions and production requirements. A small inconsistency can affect the appearance and coordination of the entire project. NESS works with project drawings, specifications, reference products and material requirements to review details before production, helping reduce production risks and maintain consistency across approved samples and production batches.

From Specification to Shipment

A controlled manufacturing process for project furniture.

We follow a structured process to turn project requirements into production-ready furniture. Our team can review drawings and specifications, confirm materials and finishes, develop samples, organize batch production, carry out quality control, and prepare furniture for project delivery. Each stage is coordinated around the approved specifications and samples to help maintain consistency throughout the project.

Our Project Workflow

Project Brief → Drawing & Specification Review → Material & Finish Confirmation → Sample / Mock-up → Approval → Batch Production → Quality Control → Packing & Identification → Shipment The process can be adapted to the requirements of each project, with key specifications, materials, finishes and samples confirmed before mass production.
